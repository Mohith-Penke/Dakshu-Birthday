Happy Birthday, My Bubu… ❤️🎂

Today is your day, Dakshayini.

And before I say anything else, I just want to tell you something very simple…

I hope you have the most beautiful birthday.

I hope this new year of your life brings you so much happiness, peace, success, and everything your heart wishes for. I hope you keep smiling, keep growing, keep chasing your dreams, and most importantly, I hope you always remain the beautiful person you are.

And I hope that somewhere in all the happiness of today, you remember that there is someone who is genuinely very, very happy that you were born.

That someone is me. ❤️

I don't really know if a letter, a website, or even a thousand words could ever explain how much you mean to me. But today, on your birthday, I want to try.

Because you slowly became such a beautiful part of my life.

My happiest moments, my stupid conversations, my biggest smiles, my emotional days — somehow, you became a part of all of them.

And I still remember September 24, 2023 — the day you proposed to me. ❤️

I don't think either of us knew that one day would become the beginning of so many memories, emotions, fights, smiles, and beautiful moments.

We had no idea how much our story would grow from that one day.

And now, every 24th has become special to me because it became ours.

Every month, we wish each other on the 24th, carrying a little reminder of where our story began.

And on September 24, 2026, it will already be three years since that beautiful beginning.

Three years of us.

Three years of memories.

Three years of being part of each other's lives.

And when I think about everything that happened between that first day and today, I realize how much we've been through together.

Our relationship was never perfect.

We've had our fights — a lot of them.

Sometimes they're small, sometimes they're really big.

There have been moments when we've been angry, hurt, frustrated, and even scared that maybe this time we'd actually lose each other.

But somehow, after everything, we always find our way back to each other.

And honestly, I think that's one of the things that makes our story special.

We're not perfect. We're real. ❤️

And Bubu, there's something about you that I don't think I tell you enough.

I really, really love your presence.

Not just when we're chatting.

Not just when we're talking.

Just having you around makes me happy.

When I'm beside you, I feel incredibly comfortable. It's a feeling I can't properly explain.

Sometimes I don't even need to talk to you.

I can just sit beside you and look at you.

And honestly…

I could just keep looking at you.

There's something about you that makes me feel peaceful and happy at the same time.

I love your childish side too.

I love the way you become completely yourself around me — the silly things you do, the little ways you behave like a kid sometimes, the random things you say, and all those tiny things you probably don't even realize I notice.

I never want you to feel like you have to hide that side of yourself with me.

I love the version of you that feels safe enough to be childish, silly, annoying, cute, emotional, and completely yourself.

Because that's my Bubu.

And that's the Bubu I love. ❤️

I still remember our first temple date.

Maybe to someone else, it might have looked like a simple day.

But for me, it became the beginning of so many memories that I would never want to forget.

Then came September 8, 2024 — your birthday.

We went back to that same temple, took pictures again, and even recreated one of our old moments.

Looking at those moments felt like looking at our own little “then and now.”

The same place.

Two different moments.

And an even bigger story between them.

Later that day, being at your home for your birthday, celebrating with you, spending time together, and making those memories became another beautiful part of our story.

And then there's January 24, 2024 — 7:30 PM.

I don't even know how to properly put that moment into words, because some memories are too personal to be explained completely.

That was our secret chapter.

A moment that belongs only to you and me.

There were no photos.

No videos.

No witnesses.

Nothing for anyone else to see.

But I don't need a photograph to remember it.

I remember the feeling.

I remember how important that moment felt to me.

I remember knowing that we had created a memory that would forever belong only to us.

Nobody else knows the complete story behind January 24, 2024 — 7:30 PM.

Only you know.

Only I know.

And honestly…

that's enough. ❤️

Some memories don't need photographs.

Some moments don't need to be explained.

Some chapters are beautiful precisely because they're known only by the two people who lived them.

And that will always be one of ours.

Then I think about your birthday in 2025.

I remember giving you those handmade gifts.

I had put my own time, effort, and feelings into making them for you.

But honestly, the best part wasn't even giving them to you.

It was watching you open them.

The excitement on your face.

The happiness in your eyes.

The way you reacted to each little gift.

Those little reactions meant more to me than the gifts themselves.

Because seeing you that happy made me happier than I could explain.

And somewhere in that moment, I realized something very simple:

I don't just love receiving happiness from you.

I love being the reason behind your happiness. ❤️

And Bubu…

There's one thing I need you to know.

Lately, we've been fighting more than usual.

And even though I know fights are a part of every relationship, sometimes they genuinely scare me.

Because I get scared that one day, after one of our fights, we'll actually end up losing each other.

And that thought hurts me more than I can explain.

Because the truth is…

I don't want to be away from you.

I don't want you to become just a memory from my past.

I don't want to imagine my days without talking to you.

Without seeing you.

Without having you beside me.

I don't want to lose you.

I love you so much, Dakshayini.

More than I know how to express.

Sometimes I don't even know how to put my feelings into words, but if there's one thing I want you to always remember, it's this:

I LOVE YOU SO MUCH. ❤️

So please, Bubu…

Stay with me.

No matter how many fights we have.

No matter how angry we get.

No matter how many times we misunderstand each other.

Let's always find our way back to each other.

Let's fight, let's get angry, let's take our time when we need it…

But let's talk.

Let's understand.

Let's forgive.

Let's laugh again.

And let's come back together.

Let's never give up on us because of one bad moment.

I don't want a relationship where we never fight.

I want a relationship where, even after the biggest fight, we still choose each other.

Because I know that underneath every fight, there is still so much love between us.

And I want that love to always be stronger than our anger.

Sometimes I imagine our future together.

One day, when we're married and living together, I imagine us being right next to each other every day.

Waking up and seeing you.

Coming home and finding you there.

Sitting together after a long day.

Talking about random things.

Laughing about things only we understand.

Fighting over some stupid little thing and then sorting it out quickly. 😂❤️

Making our own little world together.

And honestly, I feel like even if we fight then, we'll sort it out quickly because we'll still be right there beside each other.

Maybe that's what I want the most.

Not some perfect love story.

Not a life without problems.

Just…

you beside me.

Today.

Tomorrow.

And hopefully, for all the years that come after.

I want to grow with you.

I want to make more memories with you.

I want to see you achieve all your dreams.

I want to be there for your happiest days.

And I want to be there on the days when everything feels difficult too.

When life gets complicated, I want us to remember one thing:

It's always us against the problem, never us against each other.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you another beautiful year.

I want to make you a little promise too.

I'll keep choosing you.

I'll keep caring about you.

I'll keep wanting to see you happy.

And whenever we lose our way for a little while, I'll always hope that we find our way back to each other.

Because no matter how many times we fight…

I still choose you.

And I hope, every single time, you choose me too.

Dakshayini…

I don't know exactly what the future will look like.

I don't know where life will take us.

But I know one thing.

I don't want to walk into that future without you.

I want more ordinary days with you.

More birthdays.

More 24ths.

More temple dates.

More stupid conversations.

More random fights that we'll laugh about later.

More memories that nobody else understands.

More moments where I can simply sit beside you and feel comfortable just because you're there.

And maybe one day, we'll look back at this birthday and realize how far we've come.

So today, please smile.

Enjoy your day.

Forget the stress for a while.

Forget the worries.

Just be happy.

Because today belongs to you.

Happy Birthday, my Bubu. ❤️🎂

Happy Birthday to my favourite person.

The girl whose presence makes me happy.

The girl I can be completely comfortable around.

The girl I could sit beside and simply keep looking at.

The girl who became such a beautiful part of my life without even realizing it.

I love you so much, Dakshayini. ❤️

Please stay with me.

Let's keep choosing each other.

Let's keep growing together.

Let's keep making our story.

And no matter how many chapters come after this one…

I don't want to let you go.

Here's to you.

Here's to us.

Here's to three years of our story.

And here's to all the beautiful tomorrows waiting for us.

Yours, Mohith ❤️
