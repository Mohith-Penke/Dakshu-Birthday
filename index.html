Dear Bubu,

I don't really know how to put everything I feel for you into a few paragraphs. Some feelings are simply too big for words.

But today is your day, and I didn't want another birthday to pass without telling you just how special you are to me.

You became one of those people who quietly became a huge part of my world.

Somewhere between all the conversations, random moments, little jokes, and the memories we've created, you became someone I never want to lose.

And honestly, that's one of my favourite things about us.

It's not always about the big moments. It's about how even the smallest moments feel different when they're with you.

Your presence has a way of making ordinary days feel a little less ordinary.

I hope you always remember that you deserve happiness, peace, beautiful moments, and people who genuinely care about you.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you never have to face everything alone.

I can't promise that every tomorrow will be perfect.

But I can promise that I'll keep choosing you, caring for you, and cheering for you.

Today, I just want you to smile. Not because it's your birthday, but because you genuinely deserve every reason to smile.

Happy Birthday, Bubu.

Here's to you.
Here's to us.
And here's to all the beautiful tomorrows waiting for us. ❤️

Always, Mohith
