Something I have been wanting to tell you...

A Letter For My Bubu 💌

Not just a birthday wish... but a little piece of my heart.

For the girl who means so much to me ❤️

Dear Bubu, ❤️

I don't really know how to fit everything I feel for you into a few words. There are so many memories, so many little moments, so many things that only you and I understand.

But today, on your birthday, I just want to tell you something from the deepest part of my heart.

You are not just someone who became a part of my life. You became a part of my everyday thoughts, my happiest memories, my silly conversations, my biggest smiles and even my most emotional moments.

24 ❤️

I still remember September 24, 2023. The day you proposed to me.

And somehow, since then, every 24th has become a little more special to me because it became ours.

Every month we wish each other on the 24th, and I hope we keep doing that for many, many more years.

And on September 24, 2026, it will be 3 years since that beautiful beginning. ❤️

Our relationship was never perfect. We have fought a lot. Sometimes the fights became really big. Sometimes we both got angry, hurt and scared that maybe this time we would actually lose each other.

But somehow, after everything, we always find our way back to each other.

And honestly, I think that is one of the most beautiful things about us.

“Our story isn't special because it is perfect. It is special because it is real.” ❤️

I love your childish side, Bubu. I love the way you become completely comfortable around me, the way you behave like a little kid sometimes, and all those tiny things you probably don't even realise I notice.

I don't want you to ever feel like you have to change that side of yourself with me. I love the version of you that feels safe enough to be childish, silly, annoying, cute and completely yourself.

Our little memories... 🌙

I still remember our first temple date. It may have looked like a simple day, but somehow it became the beginning of so many memories.

Then came September 8, 2024. Your birthday. Going back to that same temple, taking pictures again, even recreating one of our old photos... it felt like looking at our own little “then and now.”

Later that day, being at your home for your birthday, celebrating with you and spending those moments together... those memories will always have a special place in my heart.

And that birthday night too... all those little moments, the pictures, the smiles, the time we spent together... I still remember them with a smile.

That movie date 🎬❤️

And then there was our Kalki movie date.

Honestly, it was never really about the movie for me.

What made that day special was simply having you beside me. Sitting together, talking, laughing, sharing those little moments and just spending time with you.

Sometimes it doesn't matter where we go. If you're beside me, even an ordinary day can become one of my favourite memories.

December 18, 2024 🎂❤️

My birthday.

You invited me to your home and made me cut a cake. But what I didn't expect was what came after that.

You gave me a dinner party with your family.

I genuinely didn't expect it, and that is what made it feel even more special.

Sitting there with you and your family, having dinner, talking, laughing and simply spending that time together... you made my birthday feel completely different.

It wasn't just a cake. It wasn't just a dinner. It was the feeling that you wanted to make my day special.

And I will always remember that.

One memory nobody else knows... 🔐❤️

And then there is January 24, 2024 — 7:30 PM.

Our secret chapter.

There are no photos. There are no videos. There were no witnesses. There is no gallery that can show anyone what that moment meant to us.

But maybe that is what makes it even more precious.

Because some memories are meant to be seen by everyone... and some memories belong only to two people.

“Some memories live in galleries. Some memories live forever in your heart.” ❤️

And this one will always have a very special place in mine.

Our little secrets 🤫

We have so many memories that nobody else knows about. Little things, little conversations, little moments that belong only to us.

Sometimes while we are just chatting, one of those memories suddenly comes back.

And somehow, without even saying much, we both know exactly what the other person is thinking.

We smile. We blush. We remember.

Because those memories are ours. Nobody else needs to understand them.

There is one thing I don't think I will ever get tired of.

When you're beside me, sometimes I just want to look at you.

That's it.

Even if we have already spent hours together, I can still look at you and feel like I want a little more time with you.

I don't always need something exciting to happen. Sometimes your presence itself is enough.

Bubu, I love you more than I know how to explain properly.

I love our good days. I love our stupid conversations. I love your childish moments. I love our secret memories. I love the way we make up after our fights. I love the little things that only we understand.

And yes... even though our fights can be crazy sometimes, I still don't want a life where you aren't there.

I don't want to lose you, Bubu. ❤️

I want more birthdays with you. More 24ths. More random conversations. More silly fights and even sillier reconciliations. More secret memories. More days where absolutely nothing special happens, but somehow become special just because you're beside me.

I want to keep watching you grow, keep annoying you, keep making memories with you, keep laughing with you, and keep choosing you.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like. But if I get to choose, I want you to be a part of it.

So on your birthday, I just want you to remember this:

You are deeply loved.

You are incredibly special to me.

And you will always have a very special place in my heart. ❤️

Please stay with me, Bubu. ❤️

No matter how many times we fight, no matter how many times we get angry, I hope we always find our way back to each other.

I love you so much, Bubu.

I love you forever.

Happy Birthday, my Dakshu. 🎂❤️

— Mohith ❤️
